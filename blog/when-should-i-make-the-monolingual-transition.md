After doing a lot of immersion you'll get more interested in reading Japanese (in Japanese in general), once you start feeling that learning Japanese with English becomes seemingly harder you'll naturally make the switch.

trivial: It's easier and more efficient relatively to learn some japanese in english at the start because your english profeciency is far better than your japanese profeciency, though the goal isn't to understand one word, but learn the language which can't be in english because you need to link everything with each other and be able to think and describe everything with it.
