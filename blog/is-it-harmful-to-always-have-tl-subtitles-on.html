Is it harmful to always have TL subtitles on?

December 22, 2022 (updated November 24, 2026) — Tatsumoto Ren

Watching with subtitles is a reading activity. If you have subtitles on all the time, you have less time, or maybe no time at all, to practice listening to the target language. If you're not fluent yet, and you practice reading more than listening, your listening ability will start lagging behind your reading ability. On the other hand, having target-language subtitles on can help you learn faster.

If developing a good accent and strong listening ability matters to you, always watch raw and turn subtitles on only when you hear an unknown word. If a native-like accent is a priority, you should also cut back on reading and learn entirely through listening. If you are not as concerned about that, you could keep two kinds of shows (movies, dramas, anime, etc.): one for watching raw and one for watching with subtitles, so you still have some dedicated listening practice. And if you don't care about accent at all, maybe because you are learning your TL only to read TL books or watch TV shows with subtitles, then it is fine to keep them on all the time.

The amount of passive immersion you do also plays a role. You can allow yourself to watch more content with subtitles if you listen a lot in the background.

If your listening ability is underdeveloped by the time you reach fluency, catching up will take more effort than if you listened a lot from the beginning. But the process is pretty intuitive. To develop your listening ability, all you have to do is listen a lot over a period of time.

In my experience, neglecting listening as a beginner can be crippling for your speaking. Before I started learning English through immersion, I had spent several years not listening to any native English content and being forced to output prematurely at school. I was able to improve my listening relatively quickly after I started immersing. Today I can understand everything I hear in English, maybe except for some strong accents. However, when I speak, I don't know always how to pronounce words correctly, and I often get stuck trying to recall or guess the right pronunciation. I don't have the same problem in Japanese because I took a listening-heavy approach from the start.

Lastly, I have noticed that it is hard to let go of TL subtitles if you already have them downloaded. One solution is to decide which shows you watch raw and which shows you watch with subtitles. If you are going to watch something raw, do not download subtitles at all. I also recommend watching ongoing shows. When you watch an ongoing anime, you download a new episode every week. Because the episode is still new, there are no subtitles for it yet. Your only option is to watch raw.
