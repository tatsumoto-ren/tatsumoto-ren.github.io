Basically,
language learners have two main options:
start reading **from the beginning**,
or **put it off** until later.

****

If your only goal is to understand your target language,
read books effortlessly,
and you **do not care about speaking** or talking with native speakers,
then starting to read early is a **good idea**.
As an adult,
learning a foreign language through reading
is a lot easier than learning it through listening alone.
You can improve much faster that way.
So it is fine to start reading manga as early as after finishing
[Ankidrone Foundation](jp1k-anki-deck.html).

That said, reading early has a big downside:
it can lead to [bad phonetic habits](why-does-premature-reading-cripple-phonetic-awareness.html).
Reading biases your perception of the language.
If you do not build a solid intuitive sense of the language's phonetics first,
the way it sounds in your mind while reading will be wrong and foreign.
Fixing those habits later is much harder than avoiding them in the first place,
and it takes thousands of hours of listening.

Learning through **listening alone** is harder and takes more time.
You have to rely entirely on sound to understand what is going on.
But that **listening practice** is exactly what builds strong listening skills.
Your pronunciation also depends a lot on how well you can hear sounds and notice subtle differences.

If you want to develop excellent listening skills and pronunciation,
I recommend focusing exclusively on audio-visual input at first,
like TV shows, movies, anime, and dramas.
It is hard to avoid reading completely,
since you will still need to make Anki cards and use dictionaries,
but keeping reading to a minimum will help you avoid a lot of bad phonetic habits.

The best approach is to learn kana and kanji from the start,
but **avoid active reading practice** beyond reviewing text-based flashcards.
Your main focus should be listening all the time
and learning to understand spoken language.
Do that for the first year.
It will give you a strong foundation in the language's phonetics.
After that,
you can slowly start adding light reading.
Begin with easier comic books or manga,
then gradually move on to more difficult material like novels.

Even if you are eager to start reading right away,
never neglect listening.
Listening is convenient and easy to do.
You can do it [while doing something else](mass-immersion.html),
even in the background.
